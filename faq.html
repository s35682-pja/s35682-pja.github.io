Got Questions? I've Got Answers

My workflow is divided into four main stages: Research, Conceptualization, Development, and Delivery. First, we discuss your goals and brand values. Then, I sketch ideas and create digital drafts. After your feedback, I refine the chosen concept to perfection and deliver all necessary final source files.

I work with a wide range of professional, industry-standard software. For layout and print media, I use Adobe InDesign. For digital sketching, digital painting, and modern illustrations, I leverage Adobe Fresco. When it comes to 3D modeling and complex digital assets, I work in Autodesk Maya. Additionally, my daily toolkit includes Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, and Figma for UI/UX design components.

Turnaround time strictly depends on the complexity of the commission. Simple graphic assets, social media banners, or single illustrations usually take 3 to 5 business days. Full visual identity systems, corporate branding, or comprehensive design packages can take between 2 to 4 weeks.

For professional printing purposes, you will get print-ready CMYK PDF files with bleeds. Upon agreement, I also hand over original editable source files (AI, PSD, or Figma).
